Atomic Habits;Atomic Habits is a comprehensive guide to changing your habits and getting better every day. The book teaches readers how to create good habits and break bad ones using the Four Laws of Behavior Change. The book reveals how small improvements can make a big difference, how to focus on building systems rather than achieving goals and how to create an identity-based habit. The book also provides strategies for building good habits and breaking bad ones, and how to maintain a good habit.

Mein Kampf;Mein Kampf (My Struggle) is an autobiography and political manifesto by Adolf Hitler, combining elements of his life story with his fascist and anti-Semitic ideology. Hitler recounts his childhood, World War I experiences, and political awakening, emphasizing his vision for Germany's resurgence and racial purity. He outlines his plans for conquest, scapegoating Jews and other perceived enemies. The book is a window into Hitler's extremist views, which fueled the Nazi regime's atrocities. While controversial, "Mein Kampf" provides insight into the mind of one of history's most infamous figures, serving as a warning against extremism and hatred.
